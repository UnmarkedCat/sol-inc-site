Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, automatically: every few seconds and whenever you leave the game. There's no account to sign in to.

How do I move my game to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings (the speaker button at the top right), find Save and tap Copy save code. Send the code to yourself, for example in Notes or an email. On the new phone, open Settings, tap Paste save code, paste it and tap Replace.

Can I get reminders?

Yes. After your first daily gift, the game offers to remind you when the next one is ready, when your crews stop working and when a boost ends. Turn reminders on or off anytime with Notifications in Settings.

Why is there no sound?

Sound follows your ringer switch, like most games, and plays alongside your music. Turn the ringer on and the volume up, and check Sound effects and Music in Settings.

Why don't I feel any vibration?

Check Haptics in Settings. iPhone also turns haptics off in Low Power Mode.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset game, then tap it again to confirm. This erases your company on this phone.
